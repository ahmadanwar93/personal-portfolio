## What I learned

Design is hard, and I found that out quickly. It takes time and experience, and you have to think about things like accessibility without giving up on how it looks. I wish I’d had more time and guidance to finish it.

Looking back, the font pairing could be better, especially the weights <Ref n={1} />. Something to learn more about.
