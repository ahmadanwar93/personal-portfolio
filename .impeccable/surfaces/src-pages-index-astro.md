# Surface: Home (/) and posts (/posts/[slug]) — the formal edition

Mode: Experience on the homepage (the frontispiece leads); Read on /posts. Promoted to the main site on 2026-11-07, replacing the lattice edition (archived in .impeccable/archive/lattice-edition/).

Audience/job: Fikri and peers; arrive, take in the title page, open a post and read it comfortably. Content: the six real posts (shared MDX collection), facts fixed, wording free.

User steer (binding): elegant and formal; focus on typeface, colour, line spacing, layout and flow. Print effect with real illustration, but NOT riso. Keep the minimal private-press look, push a little more creative; hero carries the flower-like guilloche rosette from the Intaglio sketch; ink pressed into paper with the uneven, unsmooth impression of the wedding app's CSS. Avoid: generic editorial blog (cream, italic serif headline, thin rules), hard to read, wedding-invitation fancy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a finely printed private-press book whose only illustrations are generative rosettes, one engraved for each project from its own name (nine engraved forms: bloom, daisy, star, rose, lotus, seed head, leaf, sprig, fern) and printed in a second ink. It refuses the elegant-blog default of a cream page, italic headline and hairline rules: elegance comes from one disciplined typeface, true book proportions and the physical bite of ink in paper.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral rag-white paper with visible fibre (never cream), dense brown-black text ink, one ultramarine rubric ink for rosettes, initials and running heads. One family, EB Garamond, in roman, italic, true small caps and old-style figures; spaced capitals for titles. Ink is uneven: speckled voids, softly roughened edges, a faint deboss on display type and rules. No cards, no boxes; structure comes from type size, small caps, the rubric colour and printer's rules.

STORY: The visitor opens a title page, sees a rosette being printed, reads one paragraph that says what this is, then the contents. They open a post and read a chapter: headpiece rosette, a large rubric initial, measured text, plates with captions. They leave with the sense of a book made with care.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred title page. A small spaced-caps nav as the running head. The rosette (frontispiece, ultramarine, about 300px) at the optical centre-top, FIKRI below in spaced capitals, a one-line rubric beneath, then the opening paragraph with a two-line rubric initial. The contents begin below the fold.

FORM: Private Press (the user's choice, from the pick card; first on my grounded list) with the Intaglio rosette lifted in by the user's steer. seed:45d57589b2d8df84

Signature interaction: the rosette prints on load (its engraved lines draw in once) and each post's rosette carries across to the chapter headpiece with a cross-document view transition. Hovering a contents line blooms its rosette. Reduced motion: printed state only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
